    Hand Edits to the Diagrams

    The edits made by hand in drawio after a step of the .agents/skills/update-docs-diagrams skill, page by page. The rules and the steps are in README.md.

    These edits were made by hand in drawio, because each one concerns a single page and a script for it would be longer than the change:

    • The pages were renamed after the image each one replaces.
    • /lib instead of /libms, /user1 on user workspace 1, and intocps.org instead of intocps.com, as the compose files in deploy/ have them.
    • The service at / is Client on every page, the name of the client service in every compose file.
    • admin/gitlab/gitlab-integrated-install.png, admin/gitlab/gitlab-integrated-runner.png and admin/gitlab/gitlab-independent-install.png gained the Logger at /logger, which the secure server packages route. On the independent page Server 2 became narrower so the page stays under 600 units wide, where 12-unit text is still 14 pixels.
    • The WebApp picture, a bitmap with its label drawn in another typeface, is the drawio globe icon with an Inter label.
    • Labels were moved clear of lines and frames after the switch to Inter, which is wider than Helvetica. Route labels such as /logger and /auth sit between two connector lines with space on both sides, and the server tags moved left of the DTaaS mark with a clear gap.
    • developer/system/multi-domain.png was laid out again so its labels fit at 25 drawio units: the columns are wider, compose, configuration and execution are rows instead of rotated columns, and the label of each stack of cards is on the card in front.
    • A Storage box hidden under the Dashboard of the Generic column of the multi-domain page was removed. It drew nothing.

    The pages the documentation does not show follow the same rules, but they were not checked label by label for overlaps.

They are Git LFS objects and are not published with the site.

    Nine pages were added from two drawio files Prasad Talasila shared on 13 October 2026: DTaaS_new_theme_more_figures.drawio, a working canvas with several versions of most figures, and DTaaS-layers-on-devops.drawio, the source of devops.png.     The Other Example Pages, 18 October 2026

    Fifteen more pages bring every diagram of the examples to the theme. Their sources are in originals/ with the prefix 2026-10-18_: the drawio files of DTaaS-examples at baa3650, the former docs/user/examples/examples.drawio (2026-10-18_docs-user-examples.drawio), and, for the five figures without a drawio source, the image the documentation showed. Each page was drawn in drawio, about 460 to 960 units wide so its text reaches 14 pixels, and passed through the retheme, restyle and shrink steps. All of them are in NO_MARK except dt-structure-nurv-fmu.png, which draws the DTaaS platform.

    Three changes to the scripts came with them:

    • The restyle draws the largest box of a page with the boundary line only when the page carries the mark. On a NO_MARK page the largest box is a box like any other; before, the first of several equal FMU boxes got the thicker line.
    • SPELLING keeps rabbitmq-fmpy, iiwa, urinterface and kukalbrinterface in lower case, as their projects write them.
    • NO_MARK lists the example pages.

    What every page shares:

    • A subscript prints at full size after its name, so FMU1 reads FMU 1, as the restyle does on every page.
    • Titles written above or beside a figure are the label of its frame, or are left out when the page text names the figure. Bold and italic are gone.
    • The restyle draws every arrowhead as the one classic arrow, so the UML composition diamonds and generalisation triangles of three-tank, flex-cell/dt-structure.png and flex-cell/pt-dt-details.png cannot stay. A composition is an arrow from the whole to each part, and a generalisation an arrow to Endpoint labelled "Is an". The role labels put the multiplicity first ("[*] availableTwins"), so the code names keep their case.
    • The lollipop interfaces of the incubator and NuRV service component diagrams are arrows from the component that requires an interface to the one that provides it. Their legend, which explained the lollipops, is left out, and so are the UML component icons. Labels the old SVGs cut short are written out from the source.
    • The fault flash of the water tank pages keeps the error red in the light image and turns light grey in the dark one, like every icon there.
